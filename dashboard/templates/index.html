Multimodal Diagnostics Pipeline

TB-Sense AI combines clinical symptoms, audio cough patterns, and radiography image features on-device. This mitigates diagnosis error and achieves superior precision.

Audio Channel (30%)

Extracts frequency coefficients and Mel spectrograms from solicitous cough clips, processing them via a 2D deep learning CNN.

Image Channel (50%)

Processes chest radiographs through a deep EfficientNetB0 feature extractor, outputting anatomical prediction indices.

Clinical Channel (20%)

Evaluates 11 constitutional symptoms and exposures, mapping flags like blood in sputum, smoking history, and chronic cough.
